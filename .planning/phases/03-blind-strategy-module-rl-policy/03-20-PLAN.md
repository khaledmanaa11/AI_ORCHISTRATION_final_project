<objective>
The demoted "Q-table". Under D-26 the learner no longer fills a 1.7M-entry action table; it tunes
**~60 weights per role** (5 actions × 12 features) that parameterise 03-16's evaluation, which
03-18 searches over.

**Why this is the fix and not a reskin.** Run 1 gave each key ~6 visits and ended with a median
best-vs-second Q margin of **0.00000** — the argmax was arbitrary. Sixty weights receive ~170,000
updates each from the same episode budget. Generalisation stops being a hope and becomes a property
of the representation.

**The one learning-theory decision:** the thief learns **on-policy**. Off-policy + linear function
approximation + bootstrapping is the deadly triad (Sutton & Barto §11.3), and Q-learning's `max`
assumes an opponent that cooperates with the bootstrap — the opposite of a safety objective.

Output: `linear_q.py`, `weights.py`, and the final `harness.py` switch.
</objective>
