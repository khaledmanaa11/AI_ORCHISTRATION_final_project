thief. Split on that line the cop scored **0.600 on trained starts and 0.133 on unseen**, which
reconstructs the official 5/20 = 0.250 exactly. **The cop was never undertrained** — it was tested
where it had never been. "Train longer" (T4-followup-1) is withdrawn as measured false.

The fix is Kakade & Langford's restart distribution: train from a mu deliberately more spread out than
the evaluation distribution d, with **d inside mu's support**.

`distinct_start_count()` and `covers()` are the surface 03-23's pre-flight check 4 asserts against.
