<objective>
Two jobs, one plan, because they share a blast radius: the Q-key's time field, and the config
surface the whole run-2 block reads.

**Why `turns_remaining` replaces the bucket rather than joining it.** Puterman 1994 ch.4 —
finite-horizon optimal policies are non-stationary in *exact* time-to-go. Pardo et al., *Time
Limits in RL*, ICML 2018 — a deadline task is non-Markov unless `turns_remaining` is in the state.
Two independent arguments, one conclusion; and the bucket is a deterministic function of the turn
index, so keeping both would only alias the exact field against a coarsened copy of itself.
**Replace, never alongside.**

**Why the config surface is here.** `config/{police,thief}/strategy.json` has exactly three owners
across this block (outline §7): this plan declares every key, 03-19 sets two class-name values,
03-21 sets the final numbers. Anything 03-14…03-25 needs must be declared *now*.
</objective>
