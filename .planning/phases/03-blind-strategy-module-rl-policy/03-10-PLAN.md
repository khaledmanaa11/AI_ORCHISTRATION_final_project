<objective>
Close the phase against its §10.4 milestone gate, and prove the central claim rather than
asserting it.

Three of the four gates are ordinary integration tests. The fourth — "the RL actually learned
something" — is the one that can be faked by accident, and `03-RESEARCH.md` §2 names how: the
heuristic is both the sparring partner and the eval opponent, so a table trained heavily against
it will beat it on the same start positions it trained on. The eval set therefore uses held-out
seeds, and the margin is measured against the *observed* heuristic-vs-heuristic baseline for
that role rather than an assumed coin flip, because cop and thief do not have symmetric odds.

Output: four gate tests, the evaluation CLI, the no-LLM CI check, and a reconciled phase TODO.
</objective>
