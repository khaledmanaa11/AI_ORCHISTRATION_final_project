<objective>
Wire the pieces into a playable brain. 03-16 gave the evaluation, 03-17 the barrier candidates,
03-18 the search, 03-12 the thief's safety filter — this plan makes them a `BrainBase` subclass the
config can select.

**The one structural decision here:** the cop's successor is a **joint action** — a movement paired
with a barrier candidate (or with no barrier). That is what makes "the barrier the cop declares is
the barrier the search chose" true by construction rather than by a second lookup that could
disagree with the first. Rules 16 and 22 make a false barrier declaration a disqualification, so
this is a correctness property, not a tidiness one.

Output: `movegen.py`, `searchbrain.py`, a registry entry, and two config string values.
</objective>
