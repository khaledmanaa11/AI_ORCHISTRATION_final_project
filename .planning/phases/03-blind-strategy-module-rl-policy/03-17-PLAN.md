<objective>
Replace the cop's barrier rule. The current one maximises BFS distance to a **fixed corner
anchor**; that rule has **no support anywhere in the literature**, and `barriers.py`'s own docstring
concedes it was invented to fill a gap the game rules leave open.

Compounding it, post-mortem **Finding 4.3**: barrier *layout* is invisible to the policy (the state
key carried a 4-bit local mask and a count), so the cop's ceiling was bounded by this hand-written
heuristic and no amount of training could move it. Run 1's cop peaked at 0.9435 in training and
still scored 0.250 at the gate.

The replacement objective is **cycle rank of the thief's free component**, with a candidate filter
sourced from Guibas et al. 1999. Output: `barrier_candidates.py` (new) and a rewritten
`choose_barrier`.
</objective>
