  <action>
    A seeded runner that plays N full games and extracts every criterion **from the event log**,
    emitting one JSON result file. Two modes:

    - `--mocked` (default): the provider is mocked from recorded responses. Fast, free, runs in
      CI, and is what a grader can rerun without a key.
    - `--live`: one real game against the API (D-32), for latency, real spend and real decode
      accuracy.

    Extract per criterion:

    1. **Hint → inference.** Fraction of turns where a hint decoded to a non-`NO_EVIDENCE`
       `Inference`, **and** the mean absolute change in belief posterior on those turns. The
       second number is the one that matters: a decode that changes nothing has not become an
       inference. Also replay the 04-07 fixtures and report decode accuracy against their
       expected shapes.
    2. **Scent updates and decays, model locked.** Assert the logged field values follow the
       locked decay law over ≥10 turns to float tolerance, and that the handshake recorded
       matching scent digests on both peers. Report the max deviation, not just a boolean.
    3. **A hint every turn, ≤ limit, both intents, flag committed first.** Report: turns with a
       hint / total turns; max word count observed; count of each intent value; and the fraction
       of turns where the intent flag was recorded before the text (from the event ordering).
       Comms are natural-language-only: scan every outgoing payload for a numeric coordinate and
       report the count, which must be zero.

    Also record: mean and p95 per-turn wall time against `watchdog_threshold`; total tokens and
    cost; and the belief-on vs belief-off win rate over the seeded set.

    Fixed seeds, recorded in the output. A measurement that cannot be rerun is an anecdote.
  </action>
  <verify>
    `--mocked` runs to completion and emits the JSON. Two runs with the same seed produce
    identical results. Every criterion is present.
  </verify>
  <done>One command, one JSON file, every number the gate needs.</done>
