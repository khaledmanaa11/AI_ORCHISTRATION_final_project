  <action>
    `BeliefAdapter` wraps a `BrainBase` and owns the belief lifecycle. `decide(state, inference,
    opponent_field, rules) -> Decision` runs Figure 7's order and nothing else:

    1. `observe_exact` when the opponent's pre-turn cell is known (Regime A);
    2. `predict` one joint turn through the motion model;
    3. `update` with the scent likelihood;
    4. `update` with the hint likelihood, weighted by the current reliability;
    5. **then** `sample` the target cell from the posterior with the injected RNG;
    6. build the `Observation` with that `target_cell`, build the believed `GameState`, and call
       the wrapped brain.

    The order is load-bearing: sampling before an update throws away that turn's evidence, and it
    would be invisible in play. Assert the sequence with a spy in the tests.

    Also feed the scent field: emit at our own cell and, for the opponent, at the cell we
    believe they occupied — a known cell in Regime A, a posterior-weighted emission in Regime B.
    04-01 built `ScentField` to take cells or cell→weight pairs precisely so the regime choice
    could live here rather than there.
  </action>
  <verify>
    A spy asserts the six steps occur in order, once each, per turn. Two turns of a scripted
    game reproduce identically under a fixed seed. Removing the hint update changes the sampled
    cell distribution measurably (proving step 4 is not a no-op).
  </verify>
  <done>The belief pipeline runs in the book's order, and the order is tested rather than assumed.</done>
</task>
