  <action>
    `contradicts(inference, opponent_field, model, config) -> float` returning a contradiction
    score in `[0, 1]`, not a boolean — the strength of the disagreement is what should drive the
    reliability step, and a hard boolean would make a marginal reading as damning as a flat one.

    The computation is the book's: from the claimed region and the implied recency, compute the
    **expected** strength there via `expected_strength_after`; compare against the **measured**
    strength in the opponent field; scale the gap by where the field's mass actually sits. A
    claim of being somewhere with no trail, while a strong trail sits at the opposite pole, is
    the maximum score. A claim consistent with the trail is zero.

    Two guards this must have:
    - **An empty field yields zero, not a contradiction.** Early in the game there is no trail;
      accusing an opponent of lying because we have not smelled anything yet is exactly backwards.
    - **A claim we cannot localise yields zero.** A hint that decodes to a heading with no region
      is not testable against the field, and untestable is not the same as false.
  </action>
  <verify>
    The book's own numbers reproduce: a field with `0.81` in the south-east and `0.00` in the
    north, against a claim of "north", scores near the maximum. The same field against a claim of
    "south-east" scores zero. An all-zero field scores zero for every claim. A region-less
    inference scores zero.
  </verify>
  <done>The lie detector from §4.4 exists, and it says how sure it is.</done>

  <action>
    `Reliability` holds one float. It starts at the configured **prior** — D-40's fixed weight,
    unchanged — and moves:

    - **Down** on contradiction, by a step proportional to the contradiction score.
    - **Up** slowly toward the prior when a hint is consistent, never above `r_max`.
    - **Clamped** to `[r_min, r_max]`, both configured and both strictly inside `(0, 1)`.

    The clamp is not defensive coding, it is a design requirement. At `0` we ignore the channel
    entirely and an honest opponent's information is wasted; at `1` we trust a fluent liar
    completely. Both bounds must be reachable-but-not-exceedable, and a test asserts that no
    sequence of a thousand extreme observations escapes them.

    Reset at handshake, per game, per opponent. **Do not persist across matches** — rule 52 gives
    one counted game per opponent, so a coefficient fitted during a warm-up describes an opponent
    who may have changed their code before the scored game (Appendix ו §2 rule 5 permits exactly
    that). State the reasoning in the docstring.

    Add to `belief.json`: prior, `r_min`, `r_max`, the contradiction step scale and the recovery
    rate. All engineering defaults, all labelled.
  </action>
  <verify>
    A thousand maximal contradictions leave the value at `r_min`, never below. A thousand
    consistent hints leave it at the prior or `r_max`, never above. The prior is what a fresh
    instance reports. Two instances do not share state.
  </verify>
  <done>Trust is earned and lost within a game, and it cannot run away in either direction.</done>
</task>

  <action>
    `hint_likelihood(inference, reliability, board, config) -> grid`:

    1. Turn the `Inference` into an implied distribution `q` over cells — a named region becomes
       its cells, an explicit cell list becomes those cells, a heading becomes a directional
       gradient from the last believed position. **This translation lives here, in `strategy/`,
       because it needs the board** — 04-07 deliberately left it out of `services/`.
    2. Mix with uniform: `L(c) = w · [r · q(c) + (1 − r) · u(c)] + (1 − w) · u(c)`, where `r` is
       the reliability, `w` the configured hint weight, and `u` uniform. The mix means **no cell
       is ever zeroed by a hint** — a confident lie shifts the posterior and cannot delete the
       truth from it.
    3. Multiply by the decoder's confidence. Confidence and reliability answer different
       questions and both belong in the product.
    4. `NO_EVIDENCE` returns the uniform grid, which leaves the posterior **exactly** unchanged.

    Add the hint weight to `belief.json` and **validate at load that it is strictly less than
    the scent weight** (D-40). A configuration that inverts the asymmetry is a rule violation in
    spirit and a self-inflicted wound in practice; the loader refuses it by name.
  </action>
  <verify>
    `NO_EVIDENCE` leaves the posterior identical under exact comparison. A maximally confident
    hint at maximum reliability shifts the argmax toward the claimed region but leaves every
    other cell with non-zero mass. Lowering reliability provably shrinks the shift. With
    contradictory scent and hint evidence in the same turn, the posterior lands nearer the scent
    reading — the D-40 asymmetry asserted numerically. A config with hint weight ≥ scent weight
    fails to load, naming both keys.
  </verify>
  <done>Words move the belief, scent moves it more, and no sentence can erase a possibility.</done>
